# L46 - Interactive SkillAudit DAG Asset

## Scope

- Added a standalone HTML/CSS/JavaScript DAG asset for Figure 1 composition.
- Kept the five node positions deterministic and independent of random layout.
- Used the established V3.3 colors and presentation-scale node/edge dimensions.

## Interaction Contract

- Clicking a node selects it and changes its fill to red.
- Pointer drag from one node to another adds a cyan dashed directed edge.
- Nodes do not move during edge creation.
- Duplicate edges, self edges, and cycle-forming edges are rejected.
