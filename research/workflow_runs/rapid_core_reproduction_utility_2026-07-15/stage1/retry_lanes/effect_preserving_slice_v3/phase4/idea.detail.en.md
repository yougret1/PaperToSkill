# EffectSlice: Eligibility-Gated Certificates for Task-Local Procedural Effects

**Method.** EffectSlice

## Motivation
**Problem framing.** PaperToSkill can turn a paper into an executable artifact, but successful reproduction does not answer a user's practical question: which procedures are actually needed for this independently specified task, and does removing the rest preserve the benefit after execution cost and regressions are counted? The bottleneck identified in Phase 1 is a decision-validity problem. A low-cost score may look faithful inside the generated workflow while reversing the sign or ordering of the intervention's later effect on the user's task.

The closest systems stop on either side of this decision. PaperBench [arxiv:2504.01848v3] and AutoReproduce [arxiv:2505.20662v4] test or automate reproduction, while A Framework for Evaluating Agentic Skills at Scale [arxiv:2606.17819v1] measures skill utility and SkillFoundry [arxiv:2604.03964v1] constructs reusable skills. Compact-artifact systems can prune or retrieve procedures, but compactness alone does not establish that the complete artifact was beneficial, that a strict nonempty subset preserved its cost-adjusted effect, or that the claim survived a sealed family of contract and guardrail checks. EffectSlice therefore reframes the object of interest from 'a shorter skill' to 'a task-local admission certificate for a shorter skill.'

**Why now.** This gap is timely because the 2026 wave of agent-skill evaluation and compilation work supplies the missing infrastructure without yet solving the admission question: A Framework for Evaluating Agentic Skills at Scale [arxiv:2606.17819v1] makes skill effects measurable, while SkillFoundry [arxiv:2604.03964v1], ClawTrace [arxiv:2604.23853v2], and Graph-of-Skills [arxiv:2604.05333v3] make generated or compact procedural artifacts available as frozen candidates. Deterministic parsers, source maps, execution traces, and paired task scorers now make it practical to bind those candidates to explicit units and untouched confirmation runs. The contribution can therefore be tested as a reducer-agnostic admission decision rather than as another pruning algorithm.

**Why prior work stopped.**
- `arxiv:2606.17819v1` (arXiv 2026): A Framework for Evaluating Agentic Skills at Scale measured the utility of complete agent skills on tasks synthesized from the evaluated skill.
  - _Did not do_: It did not condition certification on an independently supplied user task or test whether a strict nonempty subset preserves a complete skill's positive cost-adjusted effect.
  - _Structural reason_: Its measurement object is whole-skill utility rather than a source-grounded subset with a sealed equivalence and abstention protocol.
- `arxiv:2504.01848v3` (arXiv 2025): PaperBench introduced execution-verified, granular evaluation of AI systems attempting to replicate AI research.
  - _Did not do_: It did not decide whether a paper-derived procedural subset remains beneficial on an independently locked user task after local costs and guardrail regressions are deducted.
  - _Structural reason_: The benchmark measures replication success, not paired task-local treatment effects or statistically corrected subset admission.
- `arxiv:2505.20662v4` (ACL 2025): AutoReproduce automated substantial parts of the workflow needed to reproduce machine-learning research results.
  - _Did not do_: It did not certify that fewer frozen procedures preserve the complete reproduction artifact's effect, contract, and guardrails on a separate user task.
  - _Structural reason_: Its endpoint is successful reproduction, so it lacks a positive-full-effect gate, deterministic subset units, and an untouched confirmation family for reduction decisions.
- `arxiv:2604.03964v1` (arXiv 2026): SkillFoundry constructed reusable agent skills from generated experience and evaluated the resulting skill behavior.
  - _Did not do_: It did not require the complete artifact to show a sealed positive effect before admitting a smaller source-grounded slice on an independently specified task.
  - _Structural reason_: Its contribution centers on skill construction, not on a reducer-agnostic causal admission layer with corrected equivalence, guardrail, and retained-deletion evidence.

**What changes when the gap closes.** A user can make an evidence-backed adopt, reduce, or abstain decision for one paper-task pair instead of treating successful reproduction or a compact artifact as proof of usefulness. Reproduction systems such as PaperBench [arxiv:2504.01848v3] and AutoReproduce [arxiv:2505.20662v4], and skill systems such as A Framework for Evaluating Agentic Skills at Scale [arxiv:2606.17819v1], could pass their outputs through the same task-local gate, making compactness and utility claims comparable under one explicit error budget.

## Method
**Pipeline.** EffectSlice begins with one paper, one independently locked task, a complete paper-derived artifact, and a same-scaffold no-skill baseline. It first proves that the complete artifact has a positive cost-adjusted effect, then compiles every executable procedure into deterministic source-grounded atoms and an exact dependency quotient. A reducer may propose candidate slices, but every candidate is judged by fresh paired executions, a fixed error ledger, and a complete retained-unit deletion audit. Untouched confirmation applies the same admission gate to EffectSlice, a permuted-margin control, and direct compact-artifact baselines, producing either a task-local certificate or a specific abstention reason.

### M1 Eligibility Gate
*Establish that the complete artifact has a sealed positive task-local net effect before reduction.*

1. **Freeze the task manifest** (`S1`)
   - Create a canonical JavaScript Object Notation (JSON) manifest with $`task_{cases}`$ rows containing $`case_{id}`,$ immutable input and expected-artifact references, and normalized weight; `branches` entries for F and B; versioned primary and guardrail scorer callables; contract-role predicates; plain-language threshold records for the minimum full effect, slice-to-full equivalence, and each guardrail; error-family allocations; the maximum registered-test count; parser and executor digests; and disjoint eligibility, discovery, and confirmation seed identifiers. Define an $`inference_{spec}`$ interface that receives paired case/seed rows and returns the full-effect estimate, one-sided bound, slice-to-full interval, and corrected predicate decisions. 【author decision: fix the scorer direction and aggregation, contract and guardrail predicates, missing-value policy, paired resampling unit, simultaneous-bound procedure, and error-family membership; these choices control every admission decision.】 Serialize with sorted keys, normalized paths and numeric formats, hash the manifest plus every referenced immutable file with Secure Hash Algorithm 256-bit (SHA-256), and reject execution when any digest or required field differs.
   - _Why:_ Precommitment prevents the task, units, margins, and error budget from adapting to observed slice outcomes.
2. **Execute paired full-artifact branches** (`S2`)
   - Materialize one paired-run table from the sealed eligibility identifiers, with one $`pair_{id}`$ for each task-case/seed combination and two branch rows distinguished only by $`branch_{id}` F$ or B. Reset mutable state before each branch, feed byte-identical case inputs and the same seed assignment through the frozen scaffold, and capture the artifact digest, raw scorer input and output, normalized primary score, each contract predicate result, each guardrail result, execution status, and stderr digest. Join F and B only on $`pair_{id}`$; reject duplicate, missing, retried-without-registration, or digest-mismatched rows. Emit an append-only outcome ledger plus a completeness summary that proves every registered pair has exactly one valid row per branch.
   - _Why:_ The complete artifact must be measured as an intervention on the user's task before any reduction claim is meaningful.
3. **Gate on positive full effect** (`S3`)
   - For each valid $`pair_{id}`,$ form the primary-score difference F minus B using the manifest-declared score direction, retain its task-case weight, and pass the paired vector to the frozen $`inference_{spec}`.$ This step first introduces $\Delta _\tau (F)$ as the weighted paired full-artifact effect and $L_{F}$ as its simultaneous one-sided lower confidence bound; it also introduces $\delta _{min}$ as the preregistered minimum benefit. Admit F only when $L_{F}$ is strictly greater than $\delta _{min}$ and every required contract and guardrail predicate is true under its frozen correction rule. Emit an $`eligible_{F}`$ record containing the manifest hash, ledger hash, estimate, bound, threshold, predicate decisions, and evidence row identifiers; otherwise emit $`\mathit{no\_validated\_beneficial\_effect}`$ with the failed condition and no candidate-generation state.

*The complete artifact is eligible only when the simultaneous lower confidence bound of its cost-adjusted effect exceeds the preregistered minimum benefit.*
$$ L_F = \operatorname{LCB}_{1-\alpha_F}\!\left(\Delta_\tau(F)\right) > \delta_{\min} \tag{1} $$

   - _Why:_ This eligibility rule blocks certification when the full paper-derived artifact has no validated task-local benefit.

### M2 Exact Procedural Object
*Compile auditable source units and reduce the feasible family through an exact dependency quotient.*

4. **Compile source-grounded atoms** (`S4`)
   - Parse the pinned SKILL.md into ordered leaf blocks using only explicit document nodes: heading-bounded sections, list items, fenced executable blocks, and tool-template records. Represent each $`source_{map}`$ entry as a file digest plus byte and line half-open intervals, and each executable region as a pinned file digest plus symbol or template identifier and exact interval. Intersect a leaf with these records and a single manifest-defined contract role; emit an atom only when all four members resolve uniquely. Store each atom as $`\{atom_{id}, \mathit{workflow\_step\_id}, source_{span}, executable_{region}, contract_{role},$ ordinal, $component_{digests}\}`,$ where $`atom_{id}`$ is the SHA-256 digest of the canonical tuple. Split only where an explicit document or executable boundary occurs, never merge semantically similar leaves, preserve source order, and emit $`ambiguous_{atomization}`$ with candidate matches whenever any member is absent or non-unique.
   - _Why:_ A deterministic four-way tuple makes each retained procedure auditable and avoids semantic or LLM-selected units.
5. **Build the dependency graph** (`S5`)
   - Create one vertex per frozen atom and derive candidate prerequisite edges from three executor-visible sources: declared prerequisite identifiers, references from an atom to an artifact produced by another atom, and executor dependency events captured during a full-artifact trace. Interpret $u\to v$ as 'u cannot execute correctly unless v's declared output and side effects are present.' Validate each candidate edge by replaying u from the same frozen pre-state twice, once with v available and once with v plus its uniquely attributable outputs masked while all other prerequisites remain fixed; retain the edge only when the executor requests v, loses a required input, or fails a declared contract in the masked replay. Record the evidence type and replay digests on every edge, reject self-edges, collapse duplicate evidence onto one edge, validate that every observed dependency has a represented producer, and abstain when masking cannot isolate v or an unowned hidden-state dependency appears.
   - _Why:_ Candidate subsets must remain executable and dependency-closed rather than merely semantically compact.
6. **Condense and verify the exact quotient** (`S6`)
   - Run Tarjan's algorithm on G to compute Strongly Connected Components (SCCs), assign each component a stable identifier from its sorted atom identifiers, and construct condensation graph D by replacing every cross-component edge with one deduplicated quotient edge. Order components by the earliest source ordinal and then identifier. Define $\Phi $ on a dependency-closed atom set by returning exactly the SCC identifiers wholly contained in it; define Union by expanding quotient identifiers to their member atoms. Enumerate every downward-closed quotient subset because D has at most 16 nodes, expand it with Union, and verify exact correspondence by checking closure, round-trip identifiers, canonical materialized-artifact digests, and executor dependency resolution. Treat $\Delta _{D}(T)$ and $\Delta _{G}(Union(T))$ as the same ledger lookup only after both names resolve to the identical materialized atom set and digest; otherwise abstain. Emit D, membership and inverse maps, the verification ledger, and $`\mathit{granularity\_out\_of\_scope}`$ when D exceeds 16 nodes.

*The SCC projection and inverse form an exact correspondence between dependency-closed atom subsets and downward-closed quotient subsets.*
$$ \Phi(S)=\{C\in\operatorname{SCC}(G):C\subseteq S\},\quad \operatorname{Union}(\Phi(S))=S \tag{2} $$


*Quotienting changes neither the feasible artifact represented by a subset nor its measured task effect.*
$$ \Delta_D(T)=\Delta_G(\operatorname{Union}(T)) \tag{3} $$

   - _Why:_ SCC condensation supplies the single tractability compression while preserving the feasible family and measured effect exactly.
7. **Register candidates and reserve audit budget** (`S7`)
   - Let N be the number of SCC nodes in verified quotient D and set Q to the preregistered maximum of 24 candidate-and-neighbor tests. Compile a canonical candidate predicate that accepts a sorted SCC-identifier set only if it is nonempty, omits at least one SCC from F, is downward closed in D, expands to atoms whose digests and source spans remain valid, passes executor dependency resolution, and satisfies every static contract predicate. Register the final N test indices exclusively for one deletion neighbor per possible retained SCC and expose only the first $Q- N$ indices to discovery. Store each index once in an append-only query ledger with purpose, hypothesis identifier, candidate digest, seed-block identifier, and status; refuse unregistered tests, index reuse, discovery after its slots are exhausted, or any run that would leave fewer than N untouched neighbor indices.
   - _Why:_ The complete neighbor audit cannot be traded away after an attractive discovery result appears.

### M3 Slice Admission
*Generate candidates independently of fresh outcomes and certify a locked locally irreducible slice.*

8. **Propose slices without seeing fresh outcomes** (`S8`)
   - Expose a reducer adapter with input $`{\mathit{candidate\_registry\_digest}, \mathit{prior\_discovery\_ledger}, \mathit{reducer\_state\_digest}}`$ and output one canonical sorted SCC set $T_{q}$ plus an optional reducer trace. Before invocation, allocate q, a fresh paired seed-block identifier, and its preassigned discovery error share in the sealed ledger, but reveal neither fresh branch outcomes nor scorer outputs. Validate $T_{q}$ against the S7 predicate, hash the reducer name, version, configuration, state, input ledger, and output, and append the proposal before launching any $T_{q}/F/B$ branch. Invalid, duplicate, late, or outcome-informed proposals consume their registered index and produce a rejected proposal row rather than a replacement query.
   - _Why:_ Reducer-agnostic proposal and delayed outcome access separate candidate generation from the claimed admission mechanism.
9. **Measure and filter discovery slices** (`S9`)
   - For registered query q, execute $T_{q},$ F, and B on the same fresh $`pair_{id}`$ rows with isolated state and the S2 ledger schema. At this defining step, compute the slice-to-full paired gap $G_\tau (T_{q}),$ its simultaneous confidence interval $I_\tau (T_{q}),$ and discovery margin $M_\tau (T_{q}),$ where the margin is positive only when the entire interval lies strictly inside the equivalence band of half-width $\epsilon .$ Allocate $\alpha _{D,q}$ with the preregistered predictable sequence and enforce the sealed $\alpha _{F}, \alpha _{D},$ and $\alpha _{C}$ family partition without outcome-dependent reallocation. Apply the frozen correction to every contract and guardrail predicate, using $\eta _{j}$ as the declared tolerance for guardrail j. Append raw pair references, estimates, interval endpoints, margin, error allocations, predicate decisions, and correction metadata; retain $T_{q}$ only when $M_\tau (T_{q})$ is strictly positive and every corrected predicate passes.

*The discovery margin is positive only when the entire simultaneous confidence interval for the slice-to-full effect gap lies inside the equivalence band.*
$$ G_\tau(T)=\Delta_\tau(\operatorname{Union}(T))-\Delta_\tau(F),\quad M_\tau(T)=\epsilon-\sup_{g\in I_\tau(T)}|g| \tag{4} $$


*Predictable discovery allocations and sealed family budgets control repeated adaptive queries without reallocating error after outcomes.*
$$ \alpha_{D,q}=\frac{6\alpha_D}{\pi^2q^2},\quad \alpha_F+\alpha_D+\alpha_C\leq\alpha_{\mathrm{total}} \tag{5} $$

   - _Why:_ A positive margin ties selection to downstream equivalence with F rather than to size, attribution, or internal proxy scores.
10. **Lock the slice and audit every deletion neighbor** (`S10`)
   - Apply one frozen total ordering to the surviving discovery rows and persist the selected $T_{star}$ together with the exact ledger snapshot and ordering key. 【author decision: choose whether the ordering prioritizes retained SCC count, discovery margin, or another criterion, and provide a total tie-break because this choice determines the neighbor family claimed to be irreducible.】 For every SCC c retained by $T_{star},$ construct its dependency-relative deletion neighbor by removing c and every retained SCC that transitively depends on c, yielding the largest downward-closed subset that excludes c; deduplicate identical subsets while retaining all originating c identifiers. Register and execute every resulting neighbor on its reserved index, apply the corrected effect, contract, and guardrail decisions, and require each origin c to map to a tested neighbor that fails at least one condition. Emit $T_{star},$ selection evidence, the complete origin-to-neighbor map, all decision rows, and an insufficiency abstention whenever any neighbor is missing, untestable, or not rejected.
   - _Why:_ A complete registered neighbor scan supports task-local irreducibility and turns missing budget into abstention rather than selective evidence.

### M4 Sealed Comparison
*Apply one corrected gate to controls and baselines, then report held-out decisions including abstentions.*

11. **Run sealed confirmation and matched admissions** (`S11`)
   - Materialize a sealed confirmation hypothesis ledger $H_{C}$ before reading untouched outcomes, with rows for F, B, $T_{star},$ every registered deletion neighbor, the permuted-margin control, and each frozen direct output from ClawTrace, Graph-of-Skills, and SkillRAE. Normalize every compact output through the identical S4 atom compiler and S5–S7 validity checks. 【author decision: define a baseline-neutral normalization policy for outputs lacking exact provenance or executable-region records, and preregister whether a nonrepresentable output is rejected, counted as an abstention, or excluded by an applicability rule.】 Build the permuted control by applying the manifest-sealed permutation to candidate labels within each paired discovery block while preserving branch pairing, then lock its selected artifact before confirmation. On untouched pair identifiers, rerun every $H_{C}$ row, apply the frozen confirmation correction, and admit a compact output only if F remains eligible, the output is strict and nonempty, its slice-to-full interval lies inside the equivalence band, all contract and guardrail predicates pass, and its complete registered deletion family is rejected. Emit $Cert_\tau $ with manifest, artifact, atom, ledger, and decision digests for a passing $T_{star}$; otherwise emit a reason-coded corrected rejection or abstention for every $H_{C}$ row.
   - _Why:_ The common gate makes correctly admitted compact-artifact rate and false admission the mechanism-level comparison, independent of the reducer.
12. **Freeze development and report held-out decisions** (`S12`)
   - After completing development on exactly eight existing tasks, tag the source revision, hash every manifest and executable artifact, disable configuration writes, and accept only new paper-task pair manifests whose identifiers and source snapshots were absent from development. Register at least four such pairs before any outcomes are read. 【author decision: define the eligible paper-task population, selection or sampling rule, exclusions, and the outcome-independent reference procedure that labels an admitted slice false; otherwise held-out and false-admission rates are not reproducible.】 Run the unchanged S1–S11 pipeline once per pair and append one terminal row with eligibility, atomization, quotient size, candidate status, confirmation decision, abstention reason, and supporting digests. Report the correctly admitted compact-artifact rate, false-admission rate, and abstention rate over the full registered denominator, with separate counts for ineligible, ambiguous, more-than-16-node, empty, and insufficient-test cases; publish every pair-level reason code and never remove a registered pair after its terminal state is known.
   - _Why:_ Genuinely new pairs test task-local transport of the admission protocol and prevent favorable abstentions from disappearing from the claim.

## Reviewer concerns
- **Concern [non_blocking]:** Paper-pointed threat: arxiv:2604.23853v2 $(collision_{hits}).$ ClawTrace already performs cost-aware tracing and compiles preserve, prune, and repair patches for agent skills, so procedure pruning, cost attribution, and quality-aware compact skill construction are not available as EffectSlice novelty. The overlap is partial rather than exact because the retrieved abstract does not show a positive-full-effect eligibility gate, deterministic source atoms, sealed equivalence to F, or corrected retained-deletion evidence; nevertheless, because EffectSlice makes the search backend interchangeable and disclaims the reduction machinery, its claim stands or falls on whether the eligibility-and-certificate layer yields a distinct held-out decision advantage when ClawTrace, Graph-of-Skills, and SkillRAE outputs are submitted to the same sealed test.
  - **Response:** The defense is anchored in $core_{mechanism},$ which makes every search backend interchangeable and submits EffectSlice plus direct ClawTrace, Graph-of-Skills, and SkillRAE outputs to the identical beneficial-F, atom-validity, contract, guardrail, and sealed $H_{C}$ gate. Consistent with the Phase 3 verdict rationale, $\mathit{differentiation\_from\_lit}$ narrows the empirical claim to correctly admitted nonempty compact-artifact and false-admission rates under matched budgets; pruning, SCC condensation, provenance, and local minimality are explicitly disclaimed as novelty.
