# Stage 1 Direction Intake

## User Wording

帮助人快速复现相关论文的核心创新点，然后快速使用去验证对人是否有帮助。

## Normalized Research Direction

The user goal is useful but is not itself a novelty claim. The research problem is whether a low-cost, falsifiable reproduction probe can predict the net benefit of a paper's claim-bearing intervention on a user-specified task before a full reproduction is attempted.

## Scope

- Stage 1 only.
- Reuse the existing PaperToSkill real-reuse tasks, assets, scorers, skills, summaries, source maps, and human-fidelity packets where scientifically valid.
- Do not modify the manuscript, existing code, existing experiment results, or project memory.
- Stage 1 workflow artifacts may be written under this run root.
- Related comparison repositories may be downloaded under the project in a later experiment stage, but Stage 1 will prefer paper evidence and will not clone repositories unless verification requires it.

## Routing

Proceed. The direction is anchored to a concrete system, task, baseline family, and reusable evidence base.
