# Manuscript Evidence Manifests

The canonical results manifest for the final V3 paper is
`generated_results_manifest_v5.json` (schema
`effectslice-paper-results-manifest.v5`). It indexes the V4 and V5 evidence,
analysis and compatibility code, independent crosschecks, generated TeX macros,
and their SHA-256 digests.

The unversioned, V3, and V4 manifests are retained only as historical derived
artifacts. They do not describe the final manuscript and must not be used to
audit its reported counts or decisions.

Regenerate the canonical manifest and `paper/effectslice_aaai/generated_results_v3.tex`
from accepted summaries with `build_paper_results_v5.py`.
