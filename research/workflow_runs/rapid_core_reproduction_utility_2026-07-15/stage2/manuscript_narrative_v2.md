# EffectSlice Manuscript Narrative V2

## Recommended paper shape

Position EffectSlice as a run-level admission test for paper-derived procedural
skills, not as a claim that shorter skills are generally better. A paper-to-skill
system may propose a source-grounded strict subset, but the subset is admitted
only if fresh API agent runs show that the full artifact helps, the subset
preserves that help, and the subset remains beneficial over no artifact.

## Alternatives rejected

1. **Positive compression paper.** Rejected because early confirmation-v2 runs
   already falsify uniform task-local preservation. The paper must not retain the
   old single-patch admission claim.
2. **Ordinary-user benefit paper.** Rejected because no human participants,
   completion-time outcomes, or cognitive-load measurements exist. API-first
   commodity-device execution is an operational setting, not a user-benefit
   result.
3. **Run-level admission and falsification paper.** Selected because every
   central claim can be traced to the frozen protocol, 108 registered provider
   conversations, final-only private scoring, and run-level exact bounds.

## Central claim

Deterministic hidden cases evaluated on one adaptively revised patch do not
establish that a paper-derived artifact reliably changes fresh agent behavior.
EffectSlice separates candidate construction from admission by combining
source binding, dependency-closure deletion audit, write-once evidence,
final-only private scoring, and exact prevalence bounds over registered B/F/S
replicate blocks.

## Contributions

1. A formal task-local substitution decision for paper-derived procedural
   artifacts: full-artifact benefit, slice preservation, and slice benefit are
   separate run-level hypotheses.
2. A leakage-resistant evaluation protocol in which hidden cases are clustered
   checks within one condition run, private scoring occurs at most once after a
   patch is fixed, and the model never receives private feedback.
3. An executable API-first study with two paper-specific implementation tasks,
   18 matched B/F/S blocks per task, 54 provider conversations per task, and
   negative outcomes retained rather than optimized away.

## Evidence map

- Candidate provenance and deletion audit: source maps, slice registries, and
  discovery summaries.
- Pre-run commitment: local source commit `6463d31`, active family and case
  registry SHA-256 values, plus minimal public Git preregistration tags.
- Confirmation evidence: `experiment_results/confirmation_v2/**`, which is
  write-once and excludes the old interactive confirmation lineage.
- Statistical evidence: `derived/confirmation_v2/*_summary.json`; denominator
  18 matched replicate blocks per task. The 64 hidden cases define each
  condition score and are never treated as 64 independent observations.
- Paper numbers: `logs/{baseline,research,ablation}_summary.json`, final figures,
  and generated LaTeX macros derived from those JSON files.

## Required non-claims

- No cross-paper or cross-provider effectiveness estimate.
- No human-user benefit, time saving, or cognitive-load claim.
- No local-model, quantization, or GPU result.
- No global minimality of the selected slice.
- No superiority over other reducers without direct matched runs.
- The service is described as DeepSeek V3.2 under the user/provider label; the
  exact request alias `deepseek-v4-flash` and returned model field are disclosed.

## Manuscript structure

1. Introduction: the single-patch confirmation problem and paper-to-skill use
   case.
2. Related work: paper reproduction, agent skills, reduction, and adaptive
   holdout reuse.
3. Problem: distinguish condition runs, matched B/F/S replicate blocks, and
   clustered hidden checks.
4. Method: eligibility, source binding, deletion audit, final-only run-level
   confirmation, and abstention.
5. Setup: two paper-specific tasks, DeepSeek vendor API, ordinary-PC execution,
   frozen schedules, retries, and integrity bindings.
6. Results: condition success rates, three paired indicators, exact lower
   bounds, and comparison with excluded contaminated development evidence.
7. Discussion and limitations: what failure to admit means, external validity,
   provider drift, task-contract sensitivity, and missing user study.
